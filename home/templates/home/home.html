AI support, right where you get stuck

Not a chatbot bolted on the side. The help sits inside the question you are answering, and it knows which question that is.

✅

Marking that does the algebra

Answer with 3/4, 0.75 or 2x(x+1) and it is checked as maths, not as text. Equivalent forms are marked correct; only genuinely different answers are marked wrong.

💬

Ask about this question

Type what is confusing you and the tutor answers with the question in front of it, drawing on the site's own notes for that topic — so the reply matches how the topic is taught here.

💡

Hints before answers

A nudge first, the worked solution after a couple of honest attempts. Hints cost a little of the mark, so there is a reason to think first — and no reason to stay stuck.

New

Photograph your working

Maths happens on paper. Typing one final answer hides the method — and the method is where most marks are won and lost. So show it your page.

Step 1

Work it out on paper

Exactly as you would in the exam hall. Graphs, diagrams and crossings-out included.

Step 2

Scan the code with your phone

The site is on your laptop, the camera is in your pocket. Scan, snap, send — no logging in again on the phone.

Step 3

Get comments on your method

Where the reasoning holds up, where a line does not follow, whether that sketched graph behaves. Hand-drawn graphs are read too.

It comments — it never hands over the answer, and it never changes your marks. Photos are private to you, are not published anywhere on the site, can be deleted by you at any time, and are removed automatically after 90 days.
